FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear Answers Before You Start.

What does your false ceiling pricing cover, and are there hidden "patti" multipliers?
Our comprehensive false ceiling packages (for Saint-Gobain Gyproc and Sakarni POP) include original 0.55mm rust-proof GI framing at 18" grid spacing, genuine ceiling boards, imported joint compound, and 50mm fiber tape. Unlike typical local contractors who inflate final bills by billing vertical drops at 1.5x to 2x "patti" multipliers, Renowix bills on strict, transparent 1:1 linear measurements agreed upfront in your locked written BOQ.
Do you take single-room false ceiling jobs in Noida?
Yes. Whether you are redoing a single master bedroom, your drawing/dining hall, or an entire 2/3/4 BHK apartment, our engineering team handles it with the exact same laser-level precision, RWA compliance, and written warranty.
How long does work take, and how do you handle dust in occupied flats?
Standard Gypsum ceilings take 4 to 6 working days; sculpted POP ceilings take 7 to 10 days to allow proper hand-curing. For occupied apartments, we deploy heavy 120-micron zip dust barriers, mask all furniture with protective plastic film, and conduct vacuum-assisted dry sanding during official society work hours (10:00 AM – 5:30 PM).
Are electrical wiring, lights, and painting included?
Our base structure quote covers the complete false ceiling framework. Electrical conduits, premium fire-retardant wiring, COB spotlights, magnetic profile lights, and premium Asian Paints Royale finishes can be bundled as transparent, itemised line items in your locked BOQ so you have full control over brand selections.
